What BetterYTS is

  • A search front end and API for public YouTube content, with community add-ons (SponsorBlock, DeArrow, Return YouTube Dislike, LRCLIB), and tools for playing music in VRChat worlds.
  • BetterYTS is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by YouTube, Google, Spotify, TikTok or VRChat. Their names are used only to describe what the service works with.
  • It's provided free, as is, and can change or stop at any time. Features marked beta may change without notice.

Linking your accounts

  • You can choose to sign in to Google (YouTube), Spotify or TikTok to send your own playlists or videos to a VRChat world. Linking is always optional and only happens when you enter a world's code and sign in yourself.
  • We only request read access: YouTube playlists and likes, Spotify playlists and Liked Songs, or your TikTok display name and public video list (user.info.basic and video.list). We never post, upload, follow, like or change anything on your accounts.
  • Only link accounts that are yours. What we keep and for how long is set out in the Privacy Policy. You can unlink and delete it at any time.
  • Your use of those accounts is also governed by their own terms: YouTube Terms of Service, Spotify Terms of Use and TikTok Terms of Service.
  • TikTok videos are only listed, using TikTok's own titles, covers and embed links. BetterYTS doesn't download, copy or re-host TikTok videos.
