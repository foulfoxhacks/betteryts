    Use it in VRChat

    Paste this into any world's video player. It plays the top result for your search — no menus, no ads.

    /search?q=your search here

    Tip: add &n=2 for the 2nd result, or &maxlen=600 to skip anything over 10 minutes. VRChat needs Allow Untrusted URLs turned on for custom domains. All options →
