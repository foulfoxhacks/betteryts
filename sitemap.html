JSON API

Free, keyless, CORS-enabled. Not indexed by search engines.

  • /apiIndexMachine-readable endpoint list.
  • /api/searchSearchResults with ads stripped, paginated.
  • /api/video/{id}VideoDetails + all community add-ons.
  • /api/segments/{id}SponsorBlockSkip segments, hash-prefix lookup.
  • /api/dislikes/{id}Return YouTube DislikeLikes & estimated dislikes.
  • /api/brandingDeArrowCommunity titles & thumbnails.
  • /api/suggestAutocompletePlain or OpenSearch format.
  • /api/playlist/{id}PlaylistsA YouTube playlist's videos, paginated.
  • /api/channel/{id}ChannelsVideos, live, Shorts and playlists tabs.
  • /api/roomsWatch partiesSynced playback over WebSockets.
