Privacy

What BetterYTS keeps, why, for how long, and how to see or delete it. Last updated 2026-11-20.

The short version

  • No accounts. Your library, history, settings and plugins live in your browser (or on your phone in the app), not on our servers.
  • When you sign in to Google or Spotify to import playlists, it happens in your browser or app. We never store your passwords or account tokens; the website signs out of Google right after an import.
  • Linking music into VRChat is opt-in. By default nothing is kept after that visit. If you choose to save it, we keep only song IDs, titles and channels, for up to 30 days, and you can delete it any time.

The website and API

  • Searches and requests are sent to YouTube (and the add-on services below) to answer them. Responses may be cached briefly by Cloudflare. We don't build profiles or sell anything.
  • Request logs: Cloudflare keeps request logs for this site (the address requested, time, status, and the network details Cloudflare records) for a few days, visible only to the site owner, for fixing problems. Saved-library keys are removed from our own error logs.
  • Analytics: no tracking or advertising scripts. Cloudflare may count page views with its cookieless Web Analytics.
  • Your browser's storage holds your library, settings, community plugins and watch-party host keys. Clear your site data to remove them; Settings › Backup lets you export first.
  • Watch-party rooms keep what's playing, the queue and the names people choose, and are deleted after 24 hours with nobody in them.

Google and Spotify sign-in

  • What's requested: read-only access to your YouTube playlists, liked videos and subscriptions (Google), or your playlists and Liked Songs (Spotify). Nothing is posted or changed.
  • Where it goes: the import runs in your browser or the app and is saved there (or sent to a VRChat world if you choose). Our server only passes the sign-in along; for Google it adds the site's client secret to the exchange. Tokens are never stored on our servers. The website revokes its Google token right after the import; the app keeps its Google sign-in on your phone (encrypted) until you disconnect, and its Spotify token only in memory for one import.
  • BetterYTS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is also subject to YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy; you can remove access at Google account permissions.
  • Spotify data is used only to find the same songs on YouTube for you, and you can remove access at Spotify › Manage apps.

BetterYTS Music (Android)

  • Your library, history and settings stay on your phone. The app talks to this site's API and YouTube's player like the website does.
  • The sideloaded app checks /app/update.json every few hours for updates; that request carries no personal data.

Services involved

  • Cloudflare hosts the site, API and storage (encrypted at rest).
  • YouTube plays videos through its official embedded player; YouTube's own policies apply to playback.
  • Google and Spotify, only when you choose to sign in.
  • Add-ons, only for the videos you look at: SponsorBlock, DeArrow, Return YouTube Dislike and LRCLIB receive video IDs or song names.

Your choices and requests

  • Everything in your browser: export with Settings › Backup, delete by clearing site data. Saved VRChat libraries: Unlink in the world or delete on the linking page.
  • For anything else (access, deletion or questions), open an issue at github.com/foulfoxhacks/betteryts, or for private requests use the contact on akasammythepuppy.me. We aim to answer within 30 days.
  • BetterYTS isn't directed at children under 13. VRChat itself requires users to be 13 or older.
  • If we ever learn of a breach affecting stored data, we'll post a notice on this site and on GitHub without undue delay (within 72 hours where the law requires).
  • Changes to this page are dated above and tracked in the site's public source.
